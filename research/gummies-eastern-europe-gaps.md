# Gummy white-space scan: Western Europe → Eastern Europe

*Research date: 4 October 2026*

**Goal:** find gummy types that sell well in Western Europe (DE, FR, NL, BE, UK, IE, CH) and have no established brand yet in the target markets (BG, GR, RO, PL, HU, SI, SK, HR, CZ).

**Method:** web searches in English and in local languages (Polish, Romanian, Greek, Czech, Hungarian), covering pharmacy chains (Dr.Max, Catena, DOZ, Gemini), drugstores and marketplaces as search engines index them. The network policy blocked direct access to Allegro, eMAG, Skroutz and Heureka. "Not found" therefore means "no indexed listing found". It does not prove that nobody sells the product. Before committing money, check each item by hand on those four sites (see the checklist at the end).

## 1. Women's hormone gummies (PMS, cycle, perimenopause, menopause)

**West:**
- **Bears with Benefits** was founded in Munich in 2018 and "became the market leader in the rapidly growing gummy vitamin market in Europe within just 4 years". It sells through dm, Rossmann and Douglas in DE, AT, CH, IT, FR, ES, NL and the UK. Havea Group bought it to make it "the leading femhealth brand in Europe". Its markets do **not** include any of your 9 countries.
- **Manhaé** sells menopause gummies in France and Germany.
- **Mummy Gummy Menopause** (UK) uses angelica, chasteberry, lemon balm and B6.
- Other menopause gummies sold in the West: Estroven, Amberen, O Positiv MENO and "Hallo, ich bin menopause" (Austria).

**Your markets:**
- Poland: searches for PMS and hormone-balance products return capsules and kits only (ZOE PMS-B6, Health Labs HarmonyMe). A search for "menopause gummies" returns only HRT gels.
- Romania: Manhaé Menopause Gummies at Dr.Max, a single foreign SKU.
- No local femhealth gummy brand turned up anywhere.

**Why it's the best fit:** demand is proven in the West, there's no local brand, the buyers are women aged 25–55 who repeat-purchase monthly, and it's easy to build a brand story around.

**Possible line:** Cycle/PMS (magnesium, B6, chasteberry), Perimenopause/Menopause, Iron + C, Myo-inositol (PCOS). No myo-inositol gummy turned up in Poland either; there it's sold as sachets.

## 2. Lion's mane / functional mushroom gummies

- **West:** Holland & Barrett stocks Starpowa Lion's Mane 500mg, fourfive Focus & Energy 4000mg and Nutrigums Lion's Mane Complex. Superdrug stocks New Leaf. Trip (UK) ships to Germany. In Germany, "Vitalpilz-Gummies" (lion's mane, shiitake, reishi, chaga) sell through DocMorris and medpex. Bulk.com ships lion's mane gummies EU-wide.
- **Your markets:** Poland has capsules and powder only (DOZ, Gemini: My Herbs, Proton Labs, Doctorlife, MYQO). Czech Republic and Slovakia have **Kompava Memo Gummies** (Slovak brand, Dr.Max). Gumio (Lithuania) also makes them, but I couldn't confirm which countries it sells in.
- **Angle:** "Focus" gummies for students and office workers. This also fits as an extension of a women's line (brain fog in menopause).

## 3. Sea moss gummies

- **West:** Holland & Barrett named sea moss its best-selling product of the year. Its own-label 1000mg gummies, Starpowa, Vitawell and New Leaf are on shelf in the UK and Ireland.
- **Your markets:** in Poland ("mech morski") and Romania ("mușchi de mare") I found only raw product, powder and capsules. No gummy turned up.
- **Risk:** sea moss contains iodine, and EU authorities watch seaweed products for iodine overdose. Get a standardised, lab-tested extract with a declared iodine content per serving.

## 4. Shilajit gummies

- **West/global:** a TikTok Shop phenomenon. Clean Nutra's shilajit + sea moss gummy has sold 1.6M units and Bearvana 627K (US TikTok Shop). Shilajit gummies now appear in German online pharmacies (DocMorris).
- **Your markets:** in Poland, one Kaufland marketplace listing (made in Germany). In Romania, GNC "BetterAlt" at ~375 lei for 60 gummies, an expensive import.
- **Rules:** the EU Novel Food Catalogue lists shilajit as "Not novel **only in food supplements**". Sell it as a supplement, not as a food or candy.
- **Angle:** a well-priced, local-language shilajit gummy, probably marketed to men to balance a women's line.

## 5. Colostrum gummies (early trend)

- **West:** still early. US brands (Lemme by Kourtney Kardashian, Equip, Miracle Moo) are reaching the UK through importers.
- **Your markets:** in Poland colostrum is popular ("siara"), but it's sold mainly as capsules, powder and for kids.
- **Angle:** gut + immunity for adults. Cheap to test because Poles already know the ingredient.

## 6. Electrolyte gummies (early trend)

- WonderHydrate and Hydrate Bytes (made in Germany) are small. Forecasts put the global category at ~12% CAGR. I found nothing in your markets.
- This is a summer line extension rather than a hero product, because demand isn't proven in the West yet.

## Regulatory notes (EU)

- **Notification:** each country requires its own notification of a food supplement before sale (for example GIS in Poland), plus labels in the local language.
- **Claims:** only authorised health claims under Reg. 1924/2006 are allowed. "Relieves menopause" and "fixes PMS" are not authorised. Use authorised nutrient claims instead, for example vitamin B6 "contributes to the regulation of hormonal activity" and magnesium "contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue". Botanical claims (lion's mane, chasteberry) are still "on hold", so word them carefully.
- **Ashwagandha:** several member states restrict it (Denmark banned it). Keep it out of your core formulas to avoid country-by-country problems.
- **Shilajit:** allowed in food supplements only (see section 4).
- **Sea moss:** declare and limit the iodine per serving.

## Recommendation

1. **Hero position:** a femhealth gummy brand, the regional equivalent of Bears with Benefits. Start with a Cycle/PMS gummy and a Peri/Menopause gummy. This is the largest proven Western category with essentially no local brand.
2. **Second wave:** lion's mane "Focus" and a sea moss gummy. Both are proven in the UK and missing as gummies in Poland and Romania.
3. **Test lines:** a shilajit gummy (for men, TikTok-driven) and a colostrum gummy.
4. **Launch order:** Poland (≈37M people, Allegro) and Romania (≈19M, eMAG), then CZ/SK/HU through eMAG, Notino and Dr.Max, then Greece via Skroutz, and BG/HR/SI last.
